01

What is it什麼是無人機足球？What is drone soccer?

無人機足球是結合遙控無人機飛行與足球的團隊運動。兩隊在封閉的防護籠內對戰，每位選手操控一顆「無人機球」，目標是讓己方前鋒整顆穿過對方的球門環。 Drone soccer is a team sport that combines radio-controlled drone flying with soccer. Two teams face off inside a protective cage; each player flies one "drone ball", and the goal is to get your Striker completely through the opponent's goal ring.

一場比賽分三局、每局三分鐘，先拿下兩局的隊伍獲勝。無人機外層包覆塑膠或複合材料的球形護籠，允許碰撞攔截，因此比賽節奏快、身體對抗感強，很像空中的冰上曲棍球。 A match is three 3-minute sets; the first team to win two sets wins. Each drone is wrapped in a plastic or composite spherical cage, so body-checking and blocking are part of the game — fast, physical, and a lot like ice hockey in mid-air.

ST

前鋒 StrikerStriker

全隊唯一可以得分的人。可以守在自己的球門環裡防守。球的後燈會用對方隊色，並可掛彩帶方便辨識。The only player who can score. May also sit in their own goal ring to defend. Their rear light uses the opposing team's colour, and a ribbon may hang beneath for visibility.

DF

其他隊員（掩護／防守）Other players (guide / defend)

幫前鋒開路、卡位、攔截對方前鋒。穿過對方球門不算分；若為了防守停在自己的球門環內，會被判罰球。Clear a path for the Striker, block and intercept the opposing Striker. Flying through the opponent's ring doesn't count; staying inside your own ring to defend concedes a penalty shot.

C

隊長 CaptainCaptain

由場上選手之一擔任，是與裁判溝通的發言人；前鋒墜機時可喊暫停並更換前鋒（每局一次）。One of the active players; the team's spokesperson with the referees. If the Striker goes down, the captain may call a time-out and appoint a new Striker (once per set).

Two classes兩種級別與裝備規格Two classes & equipment specs

B.1.1

球形護籠Spherical cage

用塑膠或複合材料製作，禁止金屬。所有零件都要包在籠內、不得突出；每個開孔面積不超過 150 cm²；底部可以做成 2 cm 以內的平面方便放穩。Plastic or composite, never metal. Every component must sit inside the cage with nothing protruding; each opening is at most 150 cm²; the bottom may be flattened (≤ 2 cm) so the ball sits steady.

B.1.2–1.4

動力與遙控Power & radio

只能用電動馬達，最多 4 顆；每顆電芯充飽不超過 4.25 V。螺旋槳禁用金屬。使用 2.4 GHz 展頻遙控。Electric motors only, max four; each cell ≤ 4.25 V fully charged. No metal propellers. 2.4 GHz spread-spectrum radio.

B.1

安全與禁止事項Safety & bans

必須有失控保護裝置，一啟動就停止馬達。禁止預先寫好的飛行動作與自動定位；翻機復位（turtle mode）這類由選手啟動的功能可以使用。A fail-safe that stops the motors is mandatory. Pre-programmed manoeuvres and automatic positioning are banned; pilot-activated recovery such as turtle mode is allowed.

B.1.5

紅藍燈光Red vs blue lights

兩隊一紅一藍，燈條沿主框一圈，從任何角度都要看得見。球的後方另外裝至少 6 顆 LED，每顆球顏色都不同，讓選手認出自己的球。One team red, one blue, with the LED strip around the main frame and visible from any angle. A rear unit of at least 6 LEDs gives each ball its own colour so pilots can find theirs.

B.1.6

前鋒辨識Spotting the Striker

前鋒的後燈用對方隊色（紅隊前鋒亮藍、藍隊前鋒亮紅）。主辦單位也可以要求前鋒在球底掛彩帶。The Striker's rear light takes the opposing colour (blue for red team, red for blue team). Organisers may also require a ribbon hanging under the Striker.

B.3

球門環Goal rings

圓形、一紅一藍，固定在防護籠頂部，面向場中央並彼此平行。材質要夠堅固，又要有彈性，才不會撞壞球。可以加裝電子進球感測器。Circular, one red and one blue, hung securely from the cage ceiling, facing centre and parallel. Strong yet flexible so balls aren't damaged. Electronic goal sensors are allowed.

Rules quick reference規則速查Rules at a glance

以下整理自 FAI 2026 年版 F9A 規則，括號內為原文條號。正式比賽請以主辦單位公告及 FAI 原文為準。Summarised from the FAI 2026 F9A rules, with clause numbers in brackets. For actual events, the organiser's announcements and the original FAI text prevail.

罰球Penalty shot

前鋒對上 1 名防守者，有 10 秒可以射門，不計入該局時間。Striker vs one defender, 10 seconds, clock stopped.

警告Warning

輕微違規。同一原因累積 2 次會升為黃牌。Minor offence. Two for the same reason = yellow.

黃牌Yellow card

該名選手本局剩下的時間都不能上場。The player sits out the rest of the set.

紅牌Red card

該名選手整場比賽都不能再上場，全隊少一顆球。Out for the rest of the match; team plays one drone short.

B.8 / B.8.2比賽與開賽Match & start
  • 一場比賽分 3 局，每局 3 分鐘；局間休息約 2–3 分鐘，用來撿球、換電池。
  • A match is 3 sets of 3 minutes; breaks of about 2–3 minutes to retrieve balls and swap batteries.
  • 比賽前擲硬幣決定場邊，整場都不換邊。戶外比賽如果風的影響太大，裁判可以要求換邊。
  • A coin toss decides sides, kept for the whole match. Outdoors, the referee may order a swap if wind is a significant factor.
  • 開賽程序：確認兩隊就緒 → 裁判喊「Arm your quads」→ 約 3–5 秒後聲響開賽。
  • Start: both captains confirm → "Arm your quads" → sound signal about 3–5 s later.
  • 聲響前球離地算提前起飛，要重新開賽，並判給對方罰球。
  • A ball leaving the ground before the signal is an early start: restart, and a penalty shot against that team.
B.8.4得分Scoring
  • 只有前鋒能得分：整顆球朝對方底線方向完整穿過整個球門環才算。
  • Only the Striker scores: the entire ball must pass through the entire ring towards the opponent's baseline.
  • 由計分裁判判定，使用電子計分時也是一樣。
  • The scoring referee decides, even when electronic scoring is used.
  • 其他隊員穿過對方球門不算分，但也不會被罰。
  • A non-Striker going through the opponent's ring scores nothing, and is not penalised.
  • 得分後全隊要回到己方半場；沒回去就判罰球，接著的進球也不算。
  • After a goal all players must return to their half; failing that concedes a penalty shot and the following goal is void.
  • 對手可以阻擋得分隊伍回半場。掉在地上飛不起來的球，本局剩下的時間都不能再參與。
  • Opponents may block the return. A grounded ball that can't fly back is out for the rest of the set.
  • 前鋒可以待在自己的球門環裡防守；其他隊員為了防守穿過或停在自己的球門環內，會被判罰球。
  • The Striker may defend inside their own ring; any other player crossing or staying in their own ring to defend concedes a penalty shot.
B.8.5安全事件與換前鋒Safety & replacing the Striker
  • 球失控、損壞或無法安全飛行時，選手要啟動失控保護，落地後通知裁判並離開操作區，全隊在本局剩下的時間少一人比賽。
  • If a ball can't fly safely, the pilot triggers the fail-safe, informs the referee once it's down and leaves the pilots' area; the team plays short-handed for the rest of the set.
  • 如果倒下的是前鋒，隊長可以請求暫停：全員降落後改派另一名選手當前鋒，每局只能換一次。
  • If it's the Striker, the captain may call a time-out: all balls land, and another player becomes Striker — once per set.
B.8.6勝負判定Set & match result
  • 每局進球多的隊伍贏得該局，同分就是平手；先贏 2 局的隊伍獲勝。
  • More goals wins the set; equal goals is a tie. Two sets won wins the match.
  • 需要分出勝負時，依賽前公告的方式決定：延長賽（先進球的隊伍獲勝，即黃金進球）、PK 大賽（例如 5 輪，盡量由不同選手主射）或擲硬幣。
  • When a winner is needed, a pre-announced method applies: golden-goal overtime, a penalty shoot-out (e.g. 5 shots, different shooters where possible), or a coin toss.
B.9.1罰球的情況When a penalty shot is given
  • 提前起飛。
  • Early start.
  • 得分後沒有全員回到半場。
  • Not returning to own half after scoring.
  • 前鋒以外的隊員為了防守穿過或停在自己的球門環內。
  • A non-Striker crossing or sitting in their own ring to defend.
  • 由主辦單位賽前公告，罰球也可以集中在局末用剩下的電量進行。
  • If pre-announced, penalty shots may be taken at the end of the set with remaining battery.
B.9.2–B.9.4警告、黃牌、紅牌Warnings, yellow & red cards

警告Warning

  • 非上場人員（例如替補、教練）在比賽中進入操作區。
  • Unauthorised person (reserve, coach…) in the pilots' area during a set.
  • 輕微的不文明行為；未經裁判同意拖延比賽；開賽前球只有輕微移動（還不算提前起飛）。
  • Minor misconduct; unapproved delay; small movement before the signal that isn't an early start.
  • 飛行中的球不小心撞到地上的球，或不小心撞到人。
  • Unintentional contact with a grounded ball or with a person.

黃牌Yellow card

  • 同一原因被警告 2 次；比賽中更換上場選手；嚴重不文明行為。
  • Two warnings for the same reason; changing an active player mid-set; major misconduct.
  • 比賽中故意用手操弄球；故意撞擊地上的球或撞人。
  • Deliberately handling a ball mid-set; intentional contact with a grounded ball or a person.

紅牌Red card

  • 同一場累積 2 張黃牌；上場選手不在登錄名單上。
  • Two yellows in a match; an active player not on the registered list.
  • 非常嚴重的不文明行為；危險的飛行或動作。
  • Severe misconduct; dangerous or hazardous behaviour.
B.4 / B.5隊伍與備用球Team roster & spare balls
  • 兩隊上場人數相同，都是 3–5 人，由主辦單位公告；登錄名單為上場人數再加 2 名替補。
  • 3–5 active players, same for both teams, set by the organiser; the roster may add 2 reserves.
  • 名單最晚要在第一場比賽前 30 分鐘交出，賽事開始後就不能更改。教練不算隊員。
  • Roster due no later than 30 minutes before the first match and frozen once the event starts. Coaches aren't team members.
  • 每位選手最多準備 2 顆球，備用球不能裝著電池；只有局間休息時可以換球或換電池。
  • Each player may bring up to 2 balls; the spare must not carry a battery. Balls and batteries can only be swapped between sets.
  • 只能在局間休息時換人。
  • Substitutions only between sets.
B.10 / B.12中斷與裁判Interruptions & officials
  • 戶外比賽如果在場地中央離地 2 m 處測到的風速連續 1 分鐘超過 9 m/s，就要中斷比賽。
  • Outdoors, play stops if wind at 2 m above the centre stays above 9 m/s for at least one minute.
  • 比賽中斷後，該局要整局重賽；如果是某一隊造成中斷，由該隊判負。
  • An interrupted set is replayed in full, unless one team caused it — that team loses the set.
  • 裁判編制：賽事總監、主裁判、場上裁判，以及場地兩側各一名計分裁判。官方錄影不能用來在比賽中做判決。
  • Officials: event director, main referee, match referee and one scoring referee on each side. Official video can't be used for in-match decisions.

Event format賽制Tournament format

主辦單位最晚要在賽前一個月公告賽制，主要有以下兩種。Organisers announce the format at least one month ahead. Two main formats exist.

B.7.1

分組循環 → 淘汰賽 → 決賽Groups → knockout → final

以抽籤分組，每組建議 3–5 隊，組內每隊互相比賽一次。Blind-drawn groups of 3–5 teams; everyone plays everyone in the group.

3勝Win
1和Draw
0敗Loss

每組前 2 名晉級淘汰賽。排名依據：Top two advance. Ranking by:

  1. 總積分Total points
  2. 淨勝球Goal difference
  3. 總進球數Goals scored
  4. 仍然同分就用 PK 大賽決定Penalty shoot-out if still tied

範例：16 隊分成 4 組，共 32 場；24 隊分成 8 組，共 40 場。Example: 16 teams → 4 groups, 32 matches; 24 teams → 8 groups, 40 matches.

B.7.2

連續輪次制Successive rounds

每一輪重新抽籤，勝隊晉級下一輪，比賽場數較少，適合時間有限的賽事。A fresh blind draw each round; winners advance. Fewer matches, so it suits tighter schedules.

  • 單淘汰：輸一場就淘汰。Single elimination: one loss and you're out.
  • 雙敗淘汰：輸兩場才淘汰。Double elimination: out after two losses.
  • 隊伍數是單數時，最後抽到的隊伍這一輪輪空，下一輪要打兩場。With an odd count, the last team drawn sits out and plays twice next round.
  • 盡量避免同樣兩隊重複對戰。Repeat pairings are avoided where possible.

Taiwan · Event recap台灣賽事回顧：2025 第一屆天穹盃Recap: 2025 Tianqiong Cup

天穹盃是台灣推廣無人機足球（又稱「無人機飛球」）的全國性賽事，開放國小到社會人士參加，先舉辦分區預賽，再進行全國總決賽。The Tianqiong Cup is Taiwan's national drone soccer ("drone ball") championship, open from primary-school pupils to adults, with regional qualifiers leading to a national final.

主辦Hosts
國立臺東專科學校、台灣無人機競技發展協會National Taitung Junior College; Taiwan Drone Sports Development Association
組別Divisions
國小組、國中組、無限組（高中職、大專、社會人士混合）Primary, junior high, and open (senior high, university, adults)
隊伍Teams
每隊 3 人：1 名得分手（用緞帶標記）加 2 名掩護手3 per team: 1 scorer (ribbon-marked) + 2 support players
賽程Path
各分區各組前 3 名晉級全國總決賽Top 3 of each division in each region reach the national final
指定球Ball
總決賽使用協會指定的 20cm 級飛球（直徑約 200 mm、約 100 g）Final uses the association's designated 20 cm-class ball (≈ 200 mm, ≈ 100 g)

What's next未來展望：AI 與數位孿生Looking ahead: AI & digital twins

無人機足球同時結合空間座標、物理、控制與團隊戰術，因此也很適合當作 AI 研究與教育的平台。目前已經有團隊在規劃「AI 無人機競技場」這類研發與教育計畫。Because drone soccer combines spatial coordinates, physics, control and team tactics, it also makes a natural platform for AI research and education. "AI drone arena" projects are already being planned.

DIGITAL TWIN

先在 VR 或 3D 環境中重建球場、球和物理規則，可以安全地大量測試與回放比賽。Rebuild the arena, balls and physics in VR/3D first, for safe, repeatable testing and replay.

WORLD STATE

把每顆球的位置、速度、姿態和時間戳整合成統一的全場資料。A unified live picture of every ball's position, velocity, attitude and timestamp.

AI COACH

從全場視角判斷局勢，像場邊教練一樣調度盯防、攔截、進攻等戰術。A bird's-eye tactician that calls marking, interception and attack like a coach on the sideline.

SKILL-BASED FLIGHT

把複雜的飛行拆成一個個可以測試、組合的技能，例如緊盯防守、假動作、加速射門。Complex flight broken into testable, combinable skills such as tight marking, feints and sprint-to-score.

注意：FAI 規則（B.1）禁止預先寫好的飛行動作和自動定位。上面提到的 AI 應用屬於訓練、教育與展示用途，不適用於正式 FAI 比賽。Note: FAI rule B.1 bans pre-programmed manoeuvres and automatic positioning. The AI applications above are for training, education and exhibition, not for official FAI competition.
